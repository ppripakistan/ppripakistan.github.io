# PPRI Pakistan — Phase 5

Pakistan Provincial Reform Institute (PPRI)

Production static research website for https://ppripakistan.github.io/

## Phase 5 additions

- Research Agenda 2026–2027 with six research streams and planned outputs.
- Expanded research standards covering source hierarchy, reference dates, facts vs calculations vs interpretation, scenarios, surveys and corrections.
- Transparency & Research Integrity framework.
- Comparative Subnational Governance research framework.
- Constitutional & Legal Research Library focused on primary sources.
- Data Notes & Definitions for population, administrative geography, fiscal and governance data.
- Research Archive for publication/version history.
- Media Centre for journalists and researchers.
- Plain-language research FAQ.
- Expanded search index and sitemap.
- Institutional homepage and research-index integration.

## Additional content philosophy

Phase 5 adds material that makes PPRI function as an ongoing research institution: a research agenda, methods, integrity rules, comparative framework, legal library, data definitions, archive and media resources. Content distinguishes documented facts from analysis and hypothetical scenarios.
