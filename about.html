Institute Charter & Governance

Independent Policy Research for Subnational Pakistan

The Pakistan Provincial Reform Institute (PPRI) is an independent, non-partisan policy research organisation established to examine Pakistan's internal territorial structure, constitutional federalism, and local democratic devolution.

Core Institutional Pillars

The three foundational commitments guiding PPRI research
Pillar 1

Empirical Neutrality

Disciplined commitment to data-grounded facts. We evaluate evidence and trade-offs rather than endorsing partisan outcomes.

Pillar 2

Constitutional Rigor

Analyzing subnational reform through Pakistan's supreme law—Articles 1, 140A, 160, and 239—and judicial precedents.

Pillar 3

Civic & Academic Openness

All datasets, code, models, and policy papers are published freely under open-access principles for public scrutiny.

Methodological Standards

How we maintain credibility across all research
Standard 01

Primary Sources

All statistical claims are verified against primary publications from the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics, Finance Division, or official gazettes.

Standard 02

Competing Models

We do not treat any single institutional model as predetermined; we publish indicators and trade-offs behind Models A, B, C, and D side-by-side.

Standard 03

Cartographic Distinction

Hypothetical or analytical scenario boundaries are clearly delineated from official Survey of Pakistan administrative demarcations.
