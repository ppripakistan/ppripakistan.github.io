About PPRI

The Pakistan Provincial Reform Institute is a policy initiative focused on provincial structure, federalism, decentralization and institutional reform.

What is PPRI?

PPRI is designed as an independent research and policy platform for examining how Pakistan's governmental institutions can be structured to improve representation, administrative effectiveness, accountability and development.

Our approach: research the problem, examine alternatives, publish evidence and invite public scrutiny.

What we study

  • Provincial organization and boundaries
  • Federalism and constitutional design
  • Fiscal federalism and intergovernmental transfers
  • Provincial administration and public services
  • Local government and decentralization
  • Demography, geography and representation
