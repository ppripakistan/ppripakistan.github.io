About PPRI

Research for a more informed debate on governance.

PPRI is a reform-focused policy research institute examining Pakistan's provincial structure and the broader architecture of decentralized government.

Our scope

PPRI studies provincial reorganization alongside local government, fiscal federalism, administrative decentralization, representation, territorial governance and constitutional design. The institute does not treat one institutional model as the only possible reform path.

What PPRI does

Research

Policy papers, briefs, reports and working papers on institutional questions.

Evidence

Structured datasets with source, period, unit and methodological metadata.

Public debate

Accessible explanations, consultations and publication of research questions.

Research principles

  • Separate documented facts from interpretation and recommendation.
  • Prefer primary official sources for legal and statistical claims.
  • Show uncertainty and methodological limitations.
  • Present competing institutional models where the evidence permits.
  • Do not present a proposed territorial boundary as an official one.

Publication categories

Policy Papers provide comprehensive analysis. Policy Briefs summarize a focused issue. Working Papers develop research questions and methods. Reports document datasets, consultations or research projects. Articles explain evidence and public-policy issues for a wider audience.

PPRI publications are research products. Publication of a question, scenario or argument does not by itself constitute an official government proposal or a legal determination.
