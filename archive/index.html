1. Flagship Empirical Research Reports (2026)

Major multi-district field & econometric investigations
Report 01 · Household Economics

Income Went Up. So Why Does Life Feel Harder?

Empirical quantification of household purchasing power, real inflation, and wage erosion across rural and urban income quintiles using PBS HIES 2024–25 data.

 Report 02 · Administrative Friction

The Bribe You Can't See: Corruption as an Invisible Private Tax

Quantifying discretionary bureaucratic extortion and administrative rent-seeking on citizens and enterprises using National Corruption Perception Survey (NCPS 2025) data.

 Report 03 · Bureaucratic Burden

The Price of a Simple Task: Pakistan Bureaucracy Cost Index

Measuring citizen time burdens, transport costs, and administrative delays required to complete basic citizen transactions across 164 districts.

 Report 04 · Spatial Inequality

Same Country, Different Life: Measuring District Service Inequality

Spatial disparity analysis across 130+ districts measuring clean piped water, electricity reliability, secondary schooling access, and living standards.

 Report 05 · Fiscal Federalism

Does Money Work Better When Spent Closer to the People?

Empirical econometric evaluation of municipal expenditure efficiency, local council capital productivity, and service delivery under fiscal decentralization.

 Policy Paper · PPRI-PP-001

The Provincial Reorganization of Pakistan

PPRI flagship policy paper establishing a 10-dimensional constitutional, fiscal, and administrative blueprint for redrawing subnational units.
