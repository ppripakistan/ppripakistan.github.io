Policy Brief: Attracting Investment to Enhance Pakistan’s Dairy Exports

The report outlines a roadmap under URAAN Pakistan to transform the country’s dairy sector into a competitive, export-oriented industry. Despite...
The report outlines a roadmap under URAAN Pakistan to transform the country’s dairy sector into a competitive, export-oriented industry. Despite producing approximately 72 billion litres of milk annually, Pakistan’s dairy exports remain limited due to low productivity, high production costs, supply chain losses and inadequate processing. It recommends attracting private investment, strengthening farmer–processor partnerships, improving animal genetics and disease control, expanding cold chain and processing facilities, and rationalising taxes and import duties. These reforms aim to increase value-added dairy exports, improve farmers’ incomes and strengthen food security, with export targets of US$500 million by 2030 and US$2 billion by 2035.

A Preliminary Assessment of Flood Impact on Pakistan’s Economy (2025)

This report, prepared by the Ministry of Planning, Development & Special Initiatives, presents an initial evaluation of the devastating floods that...
This report, prepared by the Ministry of Planning, Development & Special Initiatives, presents an initial evaluation of the devastating floods that struck Pakistan in 2025. It highlights extensive damage across key sectors — agriculture, infrastructure, housing, power, and social services — with total economic losses estimated at **Rs. 822 billion**. Over **1,000 lives were lost**, **229,000 homes destroyed**, and **4 million people displaced**. The floods reduced projected GDP growth, strained food security, and disrupted livelihoods nationwide. The report underscores Pakistan’s acute vulnerability to climate change and calls for coordinated national recovery, climate-resilient reconstruction, and long-term disaster risk reduction strategies.
