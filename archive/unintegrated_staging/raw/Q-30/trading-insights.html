    Latest Trade Statistics

    The Latest Trade Statistics present Pakistan's access to global markets. Here you can find the interactive visualizations of the time series of export and import data for FY:2020-21 up to FY:2024-25. Trade data related to destination countries and countries of origin are plotted on geographical maps across several parameters. You can select the commodities you are interested in, or months and countries from the dropdown menu and navigate through the charts to find the latest information you are looking for.
