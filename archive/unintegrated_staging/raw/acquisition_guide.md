# Pakistan Data Resource Inventory — Interactive Acquisition Guide

This guide details sources requiring browser-based selection, credentials, or dynamic API queries.

## Priority Gated Sources (From Download Queue)

### Q-01: HIES 2024–25: microdata, questionnaires, manuals and reports
- **Grade**: A1 | **Priority**: P1 | **Topic**: Households / Poverty
- **Landing Page**: [https://www.pbs.gov.pk/hies/](https://www.pbs.gov.pk/hies/)
- **What to Obtain**: SPSS and Stata compressed files; PDF reports; questionnaires; codebooks; manuals
- **Access Caution**: Use survey weights and codebook. PBS noted a corrected/re-uploaded Section 6 on 16 Jan 2026; confirm you have the corrected file. Direct links surfaced by the current PBS catalogue; confirm files, questionnaires and codebooks match the selected survey wave before analysis.

### Q-02: Labour Force Survey 2024–25 microdata, report and documentation
- **Grade**: A1 | **Priority**: P1 | **Topic**: Labour / Jobs
- **Landing Page**: [https://www.pbs.gov.pk/labour-force-statistics/](https://www.pbs.gov.pk/labour-force-statistics/)
- **What to Obtain**: SPSS/Stata RAR files, annual report, questionnaire, codebooks, occupation/industry classifications and enumerator manual
- **Access Caution**: Use weights, survey strata/PSUs and the matching coding scheme; labour definitions can differ by survey year. Includes annual report, coding scheme, enumerator manual, technical/vocational course codes, occupation and industry classifications.

### Q-03: Population & Housing Census 2023 detailed tables
- **Grade**: A1 | **Priority**: P1 | **Topic**: Population / Census
- **Landing Page**: [https://www.pbs.gov.pk/census/](https://www.pbs.gov.pk/census/)
- **What to Obtain**: Excel tables, PDF reports, gazette notifications, enumerator questionnaires and technical documentation
- **Access Caution**: Compare administrative boundaries carefully across census years; note census revisions, coverage and enumeration definitions. Select the right geographic level and table definition; province/district figures and locality-level tables are not always directly comparable.

### Q-04: Agricultural Census 2024 report and national Excel tables
- **Grade**: A1 | **Priority**: P1 | **Topic**: Agriculture / Land
- **Landing Page**: [https://www.pbs.gov.pk/agriculture-census/](https://www.pbs.gov.pk/agriculture-census/)
- **What to Obtain**: Country report PDF, national Excel tables, historic table directory
- **Access Caution**: Distinguish full-count census variables from parameters collected on sample basis; use table notes. The report and tables have different levels of geographic detail; examine table-specific notes about full enumeration versus sample estimates.

### Q-05: Economic Census 2023: establishments and workforce tables
- **Grade**: A1 | **Priority**: P1 | **Topic**: Business / Firms / Jobs
- **Landing Page**: [https://www.pbs.gov.pk/pakistan-bureau-of-statistics-economic-statistics-production/](https://www.pbs.gov.pk/pakistan-bureau-of-statistics-economic-statistics-production/)
- **What to Obtain**: Excel and PDF tables; key findings report
- **Access Caution**: Counts of establishments are not the same as firm output, turnover, productivity or current job vacancies. The page links to province/district and industry/employment-category tables; use current census definitions for establishment and employment counts.

### Q-06: PBS annual and quarterly national accounts tables
- **Grade**: A1 | **Priority**: P1 | **Topic**: National accounts / GDP
- **Landing Page**: [https://www.pbs.gov.pk/national-accounts-2/](https://www.pbs.gov.pk/national-accounts-2/)
- **What to Obtain**: Excel tables, quarterly releases, annual series, visualization and press releases
- **Access Caution**: Use the most recent revised series consistently; historic estimates can be revised and rebased. Use the latest revised release and retain vintage/revision date when building time series.

### Q-07: PBS monthly Consumer Price Index (CPI) releases and annexures
- **Grade**: A1 | **Priority**: P1 | **Topic**: Prices / Inflation
- **Landing Page**: [https://www.pbs.gov.pk/price-statistics/](https://www.pbs.gov.pk/price-statistics/)
- **What to Obtain**: Monthly PDF release, Excel annexures, interactive trend chart and methodology
- **Access Caution**: Index changes and inflation rates are not the same as price levels; note CPI base year and basket changes. Monthly CPI reports include urban/rural and category detail; downloadable annexure format varies by release.

### Q-08: PBS weekly Sensitive Price Indicator (SPI)
- **Grade**: A1 | **Priority**: P1 | **Topic**: Prices / Inflation
- **Landing Page**: [https://www.pbs.gov.pk/price-statistics/](https://www.pbs.gov.pk/price-statistics/)
- **What to Obtain**: Weekly PDF bulletins and tables
- **Access Caution**: SPI covers a selected basket and is not a complete household cost-of-living measure; avoid comparing unlike baskets. Weekly item basket and market coverage; SPI is not interchangeable with CPI.

### Q-09: PBS Wholesale Price Index (WPI)
- **Grade**: A1 | **Priority**: P1 | **Topic**: Prices / Inflation
- **Landing Page**: [https://www.pbs.gov.pk/price-statistics/](https://www.pbs.gov.pk/price-statistics/)
- **What to Obtain**: Monthly release, charts and tables
- **Access Caution**: Coverage and item basket differ from CPI; WPI cannot be read as consumer inflation. Use the definition and basket/version accompanying each release; WPI measures wholesale prices rather than household living costs.

### Q-10: PBS external trade statistics and monthly commodity files
- **Grade**: A1 | **Priority**: P1 | **Topic**: Trade / Industry
- **Landing Page**: [https://www.pbs.gov.pk/external-trade-statistics/](https://www.pbs.gov.pk/external-trade-statistics/)
- **What to Obtain**: Excel monthly exports/imports/revised summary files; PDF analytical reports; interactive trends
- **Access Caution**: Check customs valuation, revisions, units, HS revisions and whether goods or services are represented. Includes commodity and partner details; trade values can be revised and may differ by customs/service-trade definition.

### Q-11: Pakistan Economic Survey 2025–26 full report and sector chapters
- **Grade**: A1 | **Priority**: P1 | **Topic**: Macroeconomics / Annual report
- **Landing Page**: [https://www.finance.gov.pk/survey_2026.html](https://www.finance.gov.pk/survey_2026.html)
- **What to Obtain**: Full PDF, chapter PDFs, statistical appendix and highlighted indicators
- **Access Caution**: Each chapter often cites a different producer and cut-off; trace figures to the source agency and note fiscal-year reference.

### Q-12: Pakistan Economy Dashboard
- **Grade**: A1 | **Priority**: P1 | **Topic**: Macroeconomics / Interactive dashboard
- **Landing Page**: [https://economy.finance.gov.pk/](https://economy.finance.gov.pk/)
- **What to Obtain**: Interactive sector dashboards; chart export as CSV/SVG/PNG where offered
- **Access Caution**: A convenient presentation layer. Preserve the underlying series name and source rather than citing a screenshot alone. Exports are available for supported indicators/charts; check individual dashboard source notes and reference periods.

### Q-13: SBP EasyData economic time-series portal
- **Grade**: A1 | **Priority**: P1 | **Topic**: Official finance / Monetary
- **Landing Page**: [https://easydata.sbp.org.pk/](https://easydata.sbp.org.pk/)
- **What to Obtain**: Interactive time series, dashboards, Excel/PDF downloads; API for some signed-in features
- **Access Caution**: SBP described 24,487 time-series and 3.11 million data points; API/My Data Basket may need sign-in. Portal supports series exploration and downloads; some baskets/API features may require sign-in.

### Q-14: Pakistan Demographic Survey 2020 microdata
- **Grade**: A1 | **Priority**: P1 | **Topic**: Population / Vital statistics
- **Landing Page**: [https://www.pbs.gov.pk/pds/](https://www.pbs.gov.pk/pds/)
- **What to Obtain**: SPSS RAR microdata, codebook, questionnaire, PDF reports and time-series visualizations
- **Access Caution**: Vital events estimates have sampling and recall limits; don't assume the survey captures every vital event. Check sampling, coding scheme and release notes; not equivalent to the larger PDHS household survey.

### Q-15: NIPS public microdata catalogue
- **Grade**: A1 | **Priority**: P1 | **Topic**: Population / Health
- **Landing Page**: [https://www.nips.org.pk/viewpublicdata](https://www.nips.org.pk/viewpublicdata)
- **What to Obtain**: SPSS/Stata files, survey reports and survey-related material
- **Access Caution**: Some data may require application or account; use survey weights and the release's anonymization/permission rules.

### Q-16: Punjab Publications and Datasets catalogue
- **Grade**: A1 | **Priority**: P2 | **Topic**: Provincial / Multi-sector
- **Landing Page**: [https://bos.punjab.gov.pk/dataset](https://bos.punjab.gov.pk/dataset)
- **What to Obtain**: PDF/Excel reports and listed surveys
- **Access Caution**: The page lists many reports with variable microdata availability; confirm each downloadable attachment and definitions. Lists Punjab Development Statistics, agriculture, education, health, prices, monthly industry/employment, MICS and specialist women/child-labour surveys.

### Q-17: Punjab Multiple Indicator Cluster Survey (MICS) 2024 and older waves
- **Grade**: A1 | **Priority**: P1 | **Topic**: Child / Gender / Health
- **Landing Page**: [https://bos.punjab.gov.pk/index.php/mics](https://bos.punjab.gov.pk/index.php/mics)
- **What to Obtain**: Key findings PDF; survey reports; microdata availability via official catalogue/MICS process
- **Access Caution**: The direct file listed is a key-findings report, not raw microdata; look for full report and microdata separately.

### Q-18: Pakistan Education Statistics 2023–24 and archives
- **Grade**: A1 | **Priority**: P2 | **Topic**: Education / Schools
- **Landing Page**: [https://pie.gov.pk/Publications](https://pie.gov.pk/Publications)
- **What to Obtain**: Annual PDF reports and tables; older annual report archive
- **Access Caution**: Administrative education statistics may omit unregistered/non-formal providers; check data year and source system.

### Q-19: School and student profiles / Education Management Information System (EMIS)
- **Grade**: A1 | **Priority**: P2 | **Topic**: Education / School administration
- **Landing Page**: [https://www.emis.gob.pk/website/CensusProfiles.aspx](https://www.emis.gob.pk/website/CensusProfiles.aspx)
- **What to Obtain**: School-census profiles and downloadable tables by school level; forms and proformas
- **Access Caution**: This portal is a particular EMIS source; verify province, year, reporting coverage and whether a profile reflects updated school records.

### Q-20: Pakistan poverty estimation 2024–25
- **Grade**: A1 | **Priority**: P1 | **Topic**: Poverty / Official estimate
- **Landing Page**: [https://pc.gov.pk/web/sdgreport](https://pc.gov.pk/web/sdgreport)
- **What to Obtain**: Official poverty-estimation PDF based on the Cost of Basic Needs approach and HIES 2024–25; associated poverty and inequality reports.
- **Access Caution**: The report is a statistical estimate, not public-use household microdata. Retain method and threshold definitions, and avoid mixing with MPI without stating the distinction.

### Q-21: World Bank Enterprise Survey 2022 — Pakistan firm-level dataset
- **Grade**: A2 | **Priority**: P1 | **Topic**: Business / Jobs

### Q-22: Global Findex Database 2025 — downloadable data

### Q-23: ILOSTAT bulk data download

- **Landing Page**: [https://ilostat.ilo.org/data/bulk/](https://ilostat.ilo.org/data/bulk/)

### Q-24: World Bank Microdata Library — Pakistan study catalogue
- **Grade**: A2 | **Priority**: P1 | **Topic**: Microdata catalogue / Surveys
- **Landing Page**: [https://microdata.worldbank.org/catalog/?country%5B%5D=pakistan&page=1](https://microdata.worldbank.org/catalog/?country%5B%5D=pakistan&page=1)

### Q-25: UNICEF Multiple Indicator Cluster Surveys (MICS) data portal

## All Other Catalog Gated Sources

- **[PKD-001] PBS agriculture statistics portal** (PBS)
  - Landing: https://www.pbs.gov.pk/
  - Formats: Statistical releases, tables and agricultural publications from the PBS statistics menu
  - Caveat: Where relevant compare with Ministry of National Food Security and provincial agriculture departments.

- **[PKD-003] Pakistan Agricultural Research Council research and agricultural publications** (PARC)
  - Landing: https://parc.gov.pk/
  - Formats: Scientific reports, crop studies, research papers and technical publications
  - Caveat: Research-trial results may not represent national farm conditions; assess sampling, sites and external validity.

- **[PKD-004] Auditor-General of Pakistan audit reports** (Auditor-General of Pakistan)
  - Landing: https://agp.gov.pk/
  - Formats: Audit reports, PDFs, audit observations and department-wise findings
  - Caveat: An audit observation is not necessarily a final finding of criminal wrongdoing; read response/reconciliation sections.

- **[PKD-006] Balochistan MICS and other provincial household surveys** (Bureau of Statistics Balochistan / UNICEF)
  - Landing: https://bospnd.balochistan.gov.pk/
  - Formats: PDF survey reports and source materials; raw data access varies
  - Caveat: The available report is old; treat as historical baseline and use the latest compatible household survey for current estimates.

- **[PKD-007] KP Multiple Indicator Cluster Survey microdata** (Bureau of Statistics KP / UNICEF)
  - Landing: https://kpbos.gov.pk/
  - Formats: Microdata, questionnaires, technical reports and survey findings
  - Caveat: Use the survey-specific report for exact year and sampling frame; do not merge MICS waves without harmonization.

- **[PKD-009] Sindh MICS 2018–19 report** (Sindh Bureau of Statistics / UNICEF)
  - Landing: https://www.unicef.org/pakistan/documents/sindh-multiple-indicator-cluster-survey-2018-19
  - Formats: PDF report and linked survey materials
  - Caveat: Full raw microdata may be separate from the report; check MICS data-access rules.

- **[PKD-010] Punjab Child Labour Survey 2019–20** (Bureau of Statistics Punjab)
  - Landing: https://bos.punjab.gov.pk/dataset
  - Formats: Survey report; data/microdata availability as shown in catalogue
  - Caveat: Use definition of child labour in the survey, not generic child employment; verify any data access conditions.

- **[PKD-011] Pakistan Meteorological Department climate and station resources** (PMD)
  - Landing: https://weather.gov.pk/
  - Formats: Weather records, rainfall/climate reports, advisories and station observations as released
  - Caveat: Station coverage and data access vary; climate normals and daily observations are different products.

- **[PKD-012] SECP data and statistics** (Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan)
  - Landing: https://www.secp.gov.pk/data-and-statistics/
  - Formats: Regulatory statistics, industry reports, annual reports and tables
  - Caveat: Coverage is limited to SECP-regulated domains and the tables actually published.

- **[PKD-013] Pakistan Software Export Board industry information and publications** (PSEB)
  - Landing: https://www.pseb.org.pk/
  - Formats: IT-sector reports, industry programmes and export/skills publications where released
  - Caveat: For export series use SBP/BOP source data and reconcile definitions with PSEB reports.

- **[PKD-014] PBS disability and functional-limitation statistics** (PBS)
  - Landing: https://www.pbs.gov.pk/disability-statistics-2/
  - Formats: Census tables and PDFs; links to NADRA administrative counts and historic survey tables
  - Caveat: Census functional limitations may not be comparable with older disability definitions or registration counts.

- **[PKD-015] National Disaster Management Authority situation reports and risk publications** (NDMA)
  - Landing: https://ndma.gov.pk/
  - Formats: Situation reports, hazard/risk reports, maps, emergency updates and PDFs
  - Caveat: Early impact figures may be provisional; separate affected people, displaced people, deaths and damage estimates.

- **[PKD-016] HEC Higher Education Statistics and university records** (Higher Education Commission Pakistan)
  - Landing: https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/universities/hes/Pages/default.aspx
  - Formats: Institutional statistics, annual reports and publications
  - Caveat: Check whether counts include public/private institutions, affiliated colleges and distance learning.

- **[PKD-017] National Achievement Test (NAT) 2023 and LaNA linking study** (Pakistan Institute of Education)
  - Landing: https://pie.gov.pk/Publications
  - Formats: PDF reports and findings by province
  - Caveat: Assessment sample, grades, domains and scaling differ from enrollment statistics.

- **[PKD-019] School and student profiles / Education Management Information System (EMIS)** (EMIS portal / provincial education authorities)
  - Landing: https://www.emis.gob.pk/website/CensusProfiles.aspx
  - Formats: School-census profiles and downloadable tables by school level; forms and proformas
  - Caveat: This portal is a particular EMIS source; verify province, year, reporting coverage and whether a profile reflects updated school records.

- **[PKD-020] Pakistan Education Statistics 2023–24 and archives** (Pakistan Institute of Education (PIE))
  - Landing: https://pie.gov.pk/Publications
  - Formats: Annual PDF reports and tables; older annual report archive
  - Caveat: Administrative education statistics may omit unregistered/non-formal providers; check data year and source system.

- **[PKD-021] Election Commission of Pakistan election results and reports** (ECP)
  - Landing: https://ecp.gov.pk/
  - Formats: Constituency results, notices, election reports and documents
  - Caveat: Check whether results are provisional, consolidated or final; boundaries shift across elections.

- **[PKD-022] OGRA price notifications, reports and sector publications** (OGRA)
  - Landing: https://ogra.org.pk/
  - Formats: Official price notifications, reports and licensing/regulatory documents
  - Caveat: A notified price is a regulated benchmark, not necessarily the price paid by every consumer after taxes/subsidies.

- **[PKD-023] PBS energy and mining statistics** (PBS)
  - Landing: https://www.pbs.gov.pk/
  - Formats: Statistical tables and publications
  - Caveat: For regulatory details, cross-check NEPRA, OGRA and energy ministry records.

- **[PKD-024] Pakistan Stock Exchange Data Portal** (Pakistan Stock Exchange)
  - Landing: https://dps.psx.com.pk/
  - Formats: Interactive share prices, trading volumes, company profiles, disclosures and financial statements

- **[PKD-025] Economic Affairs Division development assistance information** (Economic Affairs Division)
  - Landing: https://ead.gov.pk/
  - Formats: Official publications, project information, aid/loan summaries and financing documents
  - Caveat: Commitments, disbursements and actual expenditures are distinct; don't treat them as interchangeable.

- **[PKD-026] Punjab Women's Economic and Social Well-being Survey 2017–18** (Bureau of Statistics Punjab)
  - Landing: https://bos.punjab.gov.pk/dataset
  - Formats: Survey report and listed microdata if released
  - Caveat: Check microdata availability and questionnaire; the survey is older and Punjab-specific.

- **[PKD-027] PBS GIS and administrative-unit tables** (PBS)
  - Landing: https://www.pbs.gov.pk/gis/
  - Formats: GIS information, administrative-unit lists, maps and links to census/PSLM/HIES reports
  - Caveat: Availability of GIS boundary files varies; some maps may be visual references rather than open shapefiles.

  - Landing: https://psi.pbos.gov.pk/
  - Formats: Interactive GIS maps, boundary overlays and thematic maps
  - Caveat: Confirm boundary vintage, coordinate system and whether a layer is downloadable before joining external data.

- **[PKD-032] National Institute of Health Pakistan publications and surveillance resources** (NIH Pakistan)
  - Landing: https://www.nih.org.pk/
  - Formats: Disease advisories, technical reports, surveillance publications and health resources
  - Caveat: Released surveillance aggregates may not expose underlying case line lists; confirm reporting definitions.
