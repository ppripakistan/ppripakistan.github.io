Provinces of Pakistan 2026: Names, Capitals, Population and Area

As of 2026, Pakistan has 4 constitutional provinces (Punjab, Sindh, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, and Balochistan), 1 federal capital territory (Islamabad), and 2 autonomous administrative territories (Azad Jammu & Kashmir and Gilgit-Baltistan), serving a total population of 241.49 million (PBS Census 2023). This reference guide provides their capitals, Census 2023 populations, and territorial areas, and explains why Islamabad, Gilgit-Baltistan, and Azad Jammu and Kashmir cannot simply be counted as additional provinces.

Which is the Largest Province in Pakistan?

The answer depends on whether size is measured by land area or population:

  • Largest by Land Area: Balochistan is by far the largest province, spanning 347,190 square kilometres (nearly 44% of Pakistan’s total land area). However, with 14.89 million people, it is the least populous province, creating vast administrative distances between towns and service centres.
  • Largest by Population: Punjab is the largest province by population, holding 127.69 million people—representing more than 52% of Pakistan’s total inhabitants. Governed from Lahore, its demographic mass exceeds the sovereign populations of the United Kingdom, France, or Germany.

What Makes Each Province Distinct?

  • Punjab: The most populous province in the 2023 Census; its capital is Lahore. The province includes major parts of the Indus plain and extensive irrigated agricultural areas, alongside large urban centres.
  • Sindh: Capital Karachi; includes the lower Indus valley and the Indus Delta. Karachi is a major seaport, commercial centre and the country’s largest urban centre by commonly used population measures.
  • Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (KP): Capital Peshawar; includes mountain regions as well as the Peshawar Valley and other plains. Its geography ranges from high mountains in the north to warmer lowland districts.
  • Balochistan: Capital Quetta; the largest province by land area and the least densely populated of the four in the table. It includes extensive plateaus, mountains, arid landscapes and a coastline on the Arabian Sea.

These are broad geographic descriptions, not stereotypes: each province contains diverse landscapes, languages, communities, economies and local histories. For official population and area figures, use the PBS source linked below rather than unsourced lists or social-media graphics.

Constitutional Classification: Understanding the Territorial Hierarchy

In common conversation, people sometimes ask whether Islamabad, Gilgit-Baltistan, or Azad Kashmir are “the 5th or 6th provinces.” Legally, each occupies a distinct constitutional category:

1. Four Constitutional Provinces

Punjab, Sindh, KP, Balochistan: Full federating units under Article 1. Represented equally in the Senate (23 seats each) and entitled to the National Finance Commission (NFC) divisible pool.

2. Islamabad Capital Territory (ICT)

Federal Capital: Defined under Article 1(2)(b) as federal territory. It has 3 National Assembly seats and 4 Senate seats, but no provincial assembly or chief minister.

3. Gilgit-Baltistan (GB)

Special Administrative Territory: Governed under presidential governance orders with its own elected assembly, but not currently a constitutional province under Article 1.

4. Azad Jammu & Kashmir (AJK)

Self-Governing State: Operates under its own 1974 Interim Constitution Act with a separate President, Prime Minister, Assembly, and Supreme Court.

Frequently Asked Questions About Pakistan’s Provinces

Answers to common school-level, general-knowledge, geography, population and administrative-status questions. Official population figures on this page are from the 2023 Census.

How many provinces are there in Pakistan?

Pakistan has four constitutional provinces: Punjab, Sindh, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and Balochistan. Islamabad Capital Territory, Gilgit-Baltistan and Azad Jammu and Kashmir are not additional provinces in this count.

What are the names of Pakistan’s four provinces?

The four provinces are Punjab, Sindh, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (KP) and Balochistan.

What are the capitals of Pakistan’s provinces?

Punjab’s capital is Lahore, Sindh’s is Karachi, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa’s is Peshawar and Balochistan’s is Quetta. Islamabad is the national capital but is not a provincial capital.

Does Pakistan have four or five provinces?

Pakistan has four constitutional provinces. Some questions refer to Gilgit-Baltistan as a possible fifth province, but it is not listed as a province in Article 1 of the Constitution.

Which is the largest province in Pakistan by area?

Balochistan is the largest by land area, at 347,190 km² in the PBS Census 2023 Table 1.

Which is the largest province in Pakistan by population?

Punjab is the most populous province, with 127,688,922 people counted in the 2023 Census.

Which is the smallest province in Pakistan?

By land area, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa is the smallest of the four in the PBS Table 1 comparison. By population, Balochistan is the least populous. The answer depends on the measure.

Which province is the least populated?

Balochistan was the least populous of the four provinces in the 2023 Census, with 14,894,402 people.

Which province has the highest population density?

Punjab has the highest population density among the four provinces in this comparison, at about 622 people per km² using the displayed population and area figures.

Which province has the lowest population density?

Balochistan has the lowest population density among the four provinces in this comparison, at about 43 people per km².

What is the population of each province of Pakistan?

The PBS 2023 Census counted 127,688,922 people in Punjab, 55,696,147 in Sindh, 40,856,097 in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and 14,894,402 in Balochistan. See the table and official PBS source for context.

Are these 2026 population estimates?

No. The figures on this page are Census 2023 enumerations. The page’s 2026 date refers to the page’s review period, not a new population count or projection.

Is Islamabad a province of Pakistan?

No. Islamabad is the federal capital territory, distinct from the four constitutional provinces.

Is Gilgit-Baltistan a province of Pakistan?

Gilgit-Baltistan is not listed as one of the four provinces in Article 1 of Pakistan’s Constitution and has a distinct administrative and legal framework. Read PPRI’s detailed explanation of Gilgit-Baltistan’s status.

Is Azad Jammu and Kashmir (AJK) a province?

AJK is not one of Pakistan’s four constitutional provinces; it operates under a separate constitutional and administrative framework. Its status should not be conflated with that of a province.

Which provinces of Pakistan have a coastline?

Sindh and Balochistan border the Arabian Sea. The Indus Delta lies in southern Sindh.

Which province has Lahore as its capital?

Lahore is the capital of Punjab.

Which province has Karachi as its capital?

Karachi is the capital of Sindh and a major port and commercial centre.

Which province has Peshawar as its capital?

Peshawar is the capital of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (KP).

Which province has Quetta as its capital?

Quetta is the capital of Balochistan.

Where can I find a map of Pakistan’s provinces?

Use the province map or map resources available on this page and the PPRI map collection. A map should distinguish the four provinces from Islamabad Capital Territory, Gilgit-Baltistan and AJK.

Where can I download official province-wise population data?

Start with the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics Census 2023 results and the linked PBS Table 1. PPRI’s Population & Cities Observatory and Open Data Downloads provide related resources.

Will Pakistan create new provinces?

There are periodic proposals and public debates, but proposals are not the same as a legally created province. Any change must follow the applicable constitutional process. See PPRI’s overview of new administrative units in Pakistan.

What is the difference between a province and a division?

A province is a constitutional federating unit. A division is a sub-provincial administrative grouping of districts and does not have the same constitutional status as a province.

Which province is known for the Indus Delta?

The Indus Delta is in southern Sindh, where the Indus River system reaches the Arabian Sea.

Why can answers about the ‘largest province’ differ?

Because largest can mean land area, population or another measure. Balochistan is largest by area, while Punjab is largest by population in the 2023 Census.

What is the official source for the figures on this page?

The primary statistical reference is the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics’ Census 2023 Table 1, linked in the source list. The census year, units and geographic definitions should be checked before comparing figures with other publications.
