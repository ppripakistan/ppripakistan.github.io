Pending / Un-enacted Proposals in Parliamentary Registers (2026)

The following legislative instruments have appeared on parliamentary agendas but have not been enacted into law:

  • Constitution (Amendment) Bill, 2026 (Article 24A): Private member proposal regarding property restitution. Referred to committee; not enacted.
  • Constitution (Amendment) Bill, 2026 (Articles 51 and 106): Private member proposal on women and minority seat quotas post-Census 2023. In committee; not enacted.
  • Constitution (Amendment) Bill, 2024 (Article 62): Senate register proposal concerning disqualification timelines. Under consideration; not enacted.
