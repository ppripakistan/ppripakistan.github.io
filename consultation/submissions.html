Public participation

Put an idea into the research record.

Share a question, observation, reservation or reform idea that you think PPRI should examine. Keep the distinction clear: submitting an idea does not mean that PPRI endorses it.

No account required

Send an idea to the PPRI research desk.

Fill in the form and PPRI will prepare a Gmail message addressed to ppripakistan@gmail.com. Your Gmail account stays in control: review the message and press Send yourself. PPRI does not ask you to create an account here.

Nothing has been sent yet. The Gmail button prepares a message; you decide whether to send it.

What makes an idea useful?

  • A specific question rather than a general slogan.
  • A clear place, institution or policy area where relevant.
  • Sources or evidence where you have them.
  • A reservation or alternative explanation is welcome.
How PPRI treats submissions

Ideas can inform future research questions, explainers, data investigations or consultation material. They remain distinct from PPRI's own evidence-based findings.
