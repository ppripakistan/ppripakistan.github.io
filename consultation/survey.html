Provincial Reform Survey

An interactive questionnaire collecting citizen perspectives on provincial structure, devolution under Article 140A, and governance priorities across Pakistan.

Methodology & Transparency Standard

This questionnaire is a qualitative research consultation instrument. Responses help identify regional priorities, perceived institutional risks, and trade-offs. Individual responses are stored privately in your browser session and can be compiled into a research submission draft.

Section A: Provincial Structure
Step 1 of 3

1. In principle, do you support or oppose the creation of additional provinces in Pakistan?

2. What are the primary factors driving your position? (Select all that apply)

3. To what extent would empowering elected Local Governments (Article 140A) address governance challenges without changing provincial borders?

1: Not effective at all 5: Entirely sufficient alternative

4. Which institutional reforms should be prioritized alongside or prior to provincial reorganization?

5. Which single public outcome matters most in evaluating governance reform?

6. What is your home province or region? (For geographic cross-tabulation)

7. Optional: Any specific observation or question for PPRI researchers?
