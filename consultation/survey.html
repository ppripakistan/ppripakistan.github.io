Provincial Reform Survey

A questionnaire framework for collecting public views on provincial structure and alternative governance reforms.

Methodology note

This questionnaire is not itself evidence of national public opinion. Published results must disclose recruitment, sample size, dates, geographic coverage and limitations.

Section A — Provincial structure

  1. Do you support or oppose the creation of additional provinces in Pakistan?
  2. What are the main reasons for your position?
  3. What reservations or risks do you associate with additional provinces?

Section B — Alternatives

  1. Would stronger elected local governments address some concerns associated with provincial structure?
  2. Which other institutional reforms should be considered?

Section C — Priorities

  1. Which outcomes matter most: representation, service delivery, administrative access, fiscal management, development, accountability, or another objective?

Publication standard

PPRI should publish the exact questionnaire, field dates, collection mechanism, response count, geographic coverage and methodological limitations alongside any results.
