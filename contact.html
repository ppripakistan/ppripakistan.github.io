Institutional Protocols

Commitment to open correspondence and editorial integrity
Standard 01

Turnaround Standard

Official media and parliamentary research inquiries are acknowledged within 24 business hours. Research fellowship queries are reviewed weekly.

Standard 02

Non-Partisan Engagement

PPRI engages with all political parties, parliamentary committees, and civic groups on equal, transparent, and public terms.

Standard 03

Confidentiality

Civic survey responses and background consultations are strictly anonymized in accordance with our institutional research ethics policy.
