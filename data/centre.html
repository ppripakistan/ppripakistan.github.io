Executive Data Summary & Sub-National Benchmarks (Census 2023 / FY26)
Click to View Benchmarks ▼

Executive Overview: Pakistan's governance and public administration operate across 241.49 million citizens (PBS Census 2023), distributed over four constitutional provinces, one federal capital territory, and two autonomous regions. As of FY2025–26, national macroeconomic and public finance data reflect structural centralizations: the 7th National Finance Commission (NFC) Award distributes 57.5% of the federal divisible pool to provinces, yet intra-provincial allocations remain concentrated in provincial capitals (Lahore, Karachi, and Peshawar), with limited devolved funds reaching Pakistan's 164 districts under Article 140A.

Empirical data across verified observatory domains reveals significant sub-national variance. Punjab encompasses 127.69 million people (52.88% of the federation), creating single-secretariat governance ratios of over 3.11 million citizens per district. In contrast, Balochistan covers 347,190 square kilometers (43.6% of national land area) with only 14.89 million people, producing extreme geographic distance to public services. Urban Sindh is anchored by Karachi Division's 20.38 million residents across 7 districts and 25 municipal towns, generating over 68% of provincial tax revenue, while rural districts exhibit severe human development gaps under PBS HIES 2024–25 surveys.

02 // FISCAL FEDERALISM

National Finance Commission (NFC) Reform Simulator

Under the 7th NFC Award, the federal divisible pool is allocated using four criteria: Population (82%), Poverty/Backwardness (10.3%), Revenue Collection (5.0%), and Inverse Density (2.7%). Adjust sliders to test formula adjustments.
