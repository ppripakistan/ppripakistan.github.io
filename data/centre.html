Executive Data Summary & Sub-National Benchmarks (Census 2023 / FY26) ▼

Executive Overview: Pakistan's governance and public administration operate across 241.49 million citizens (PBS Census 2023), distributed over four constitutional provinces, one federal capital territory, and two autonomous regions. The 7th NFC Award distributes 57.5% of the federal divisible pool to provinces, across 144 provincial & ICT districts (168 total incl. 14 GB & 10 AJK, Admin Geography 2023).
