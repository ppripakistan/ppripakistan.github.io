Pakistan Cross-Provincial Comparative Governance & Indicators: Executive Summary

Executive Overview: Comparative evaluation of Pakistan's four federating units demonstrates asymmetric governance scale: Punjab alone accounts for more citizens (127.69M) than the other three provinces combined (111.45M). This structural demographic imbalance gives Punjab an effective legislative and demographic majority in the National Assembly, generating recurring constitutional friction over water rights, NFC divisible pool shares, and federal civil service recruitment quotas.

Balochistan presents the inverse administrative challenge: its 14.89 million citizens are dispersed over 347,190 square kilometers, resulting in a population density of only 42.9 persons per square kilometer (compared to 621.8 in Punjab). Sindh features Pakistan's highest urban concentration at 53.7%, yet exhibits intense intra-provincial economic polarization between Karachi Division and its agricultural hinterland. Khyber Pakhtunkhwa faces integrated administrative restructuring following the merger of the former FATA tribal districts.
