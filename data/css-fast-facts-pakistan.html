3. 7th NFC Award Sharing Formula & Criteria

Distribution Criterion Weight (%) Underlying Economic Rationale
Population82.0%Primary demand indicator for recurrent service delivery (education, health, police).
Poverty & Backwardness10.3%Equalization grant for underdeveloped districts (measured via multi-dimensional poverty).
Revenue Collection / Generation5.0%Incentive reward for provincial economic productivity and tax generation.
Inverse Population Density (Area)2.7%Compensates for high unit costs of infrastructure delivery across vast landmasses (benefits Balochistan).

Vertical Share: Federal Govt retains 42.5%; Provinces receive 57.5% of the Divisible Pool.
Horizontal Provincial Allocation: Punjab: 51.74% · Sindh: 24.55% · Khyber Pakhtunkhwa: 14.62% (+1% WoT) · Balochistan: 9.09%.

4. Key Constitutional Articles on Federalism & Devolution

Article Core Subject Matter Key Legal Mandate & Voting Threshold
Article 1 Territories of the Republic Lists Balochistan, KP, Punjab, Sindh, and ICT. Modifying requires constitutional amendment.
Article 140A Local Government System Mandates devolution of political, administrative, and financial responsibility to elected local representatives; ECP holds elections.
Article 160 National Finance Commission Article 160(3A) ratchets provincial share: cannot be less than the percentage in the previous NFC Award.
Article 172(3) Natural Resources Co-Ownership Mineral oil and natural gas vest jointly and equally (50-50) in the province and the Federal Government (18th Amendment).
Article 239(4) Altering Provincial Boundaries Absolute provincial veto: Any bill altering limits of a province must be passed by 2/3 total membership of that Provincial Assembly.
