How Many Districts Are in Pakistan? (164 Administrative Districts)

Pakistan has 164 administrative districts in total. By province and territory: Punjab: 41 districts, Sindh: 30 districts, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa: 36 districts, Balochistan: 36 districts, Islamabad (ICT): 1 district, Azad Jammu & Kashmir (AJK): 10 districts, and Gilgit-Baltistan (GB): 10 districts. Note: For GIS mapping and census publication, the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics (PBS) groups these into 141 mapped census district units.

2. High-Yield Constitutional Articles for CSS/PMS

Article Subject Matter Core Legal Rule / Mechanism
Article 1 Territories of Pakistan Defines Pakistan as consisting of 4 Provinces, Islamabad Capital Territory, and federally administered territories.
Article 140A Local Government Devolution Requires provinces by law to devolve political, administrative, and financial authority to elected local governments; ECP conducts polls.
Article 160 National Finance Commission (NFC) Establishes the NFC to divide divisible pool taxes between Federation and Provinces. Clause (3A) bars reducing provincial shares below the previous award.
Article 239(4) Creation of New Provinces A constitutional bill altering provincial boundaries requires a 2/3 supermajority in the affected Provincial Assembly before presidential assent.
Article 153–154 Council of Common Interests (CCI) Federal-provincial dispute resolution forum for water, energy, and shared legislative subjects. Formulates policy on Federal Legislative List Part II.

3. Key Landmark Constitutional Amendments

  • 18th Amendment (2010): Abolished the Concurrent Legislative List, devolved 17 ministries to provinces, inserted Article 140A(2) (ECP local elections), inserted Article 160(3A) (NFC protection), and renamed NWFP to Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.
  • 24th Amendment (2017): Reallocated National Assembly seats among provinces based on provisional results of the 2017 Census.
  • 25th Amendment (2018): Enacted the historic merger of the Federally Administered Tribal Areas (FATA) into Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.
