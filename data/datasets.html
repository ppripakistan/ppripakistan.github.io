Open Research Data

Datasets & Spatial Files

Machine-readable data, Census 2023 provincial baselines, official GIS GeoJSON boundaries, and source registries published under open public licenses for researchers and data journalists.

Open Access Standard

In accordance with PPRI's Pillar 5 (Open Data), all analytical tables, spatial geometry files, and metadata standards are published openly without paywalls. Every dataset includes documented reference periods, collection methodology, and primary source citations.

Data Citation Guideline

When citing PPRI datasets in academic papers, policy briefs, or media visualizations, please use:
Pakistan Provincial Reform Institute (PPRI). (2026). Open Research Datasets: Census 2023 Baseline and Administrative Scenario Layers. Retrieved from https://ppripakistan.github.io/data/datasets.html
