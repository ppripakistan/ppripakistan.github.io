Demographic Contrasts

Balochistan encompasses 43.6% of Pakistan's entire land area but only 6.17% of its population, yielding an extremely low population density of 42.9 people per square kilometer. Conversely, Punjab accommodates more than half of the national population within approximately one-fourth of the land area. Sindh is the most urbanized province (53.97%), heavily driven by the Karachi metropolitan agglomeration.
