Digital Access, Mobile & Technology

Verified PBS HIES 2024–25 digital indicators: 96% household mobile access, 70% household internet access, 50% individual mobile ownership (age 10+), and 57% individual internet usage.

Household Access vs. Individual Ownership & Use (Why the Numbers Differ): In PBS HIES 2024–25, 96% of households have at least one mobile phone and 70% of households have internet access, but only 50% of individuals (age 10+) personally own a mobile phone (Male 69% vs. Female 31%) and 57% of individuals (age 10+) used the internet in the last three months. Household figures reflect shared family access; individual figures measure personal ownership or usage and also differ from PTA SIM-count subscriptions.
