Pakistan Macroeconomic Benchmarks, Inflation & Wages: Executive Economic Summary

Executive Overview: Pakistan's macroeconomic environment for FY2025–26 reflects stabilization following severe monetary shocks, with real GDP growth projected at 3.70% by the Pakistan Economic Survey (PES) and national CPI headline inflation moderating to 10.26% year-on-year. Nominal GDP reached PKR 106.05 trillion (approximately USD 375 billion), yielding a nominal per capita income of USD 1,680 per annum.

Sectoral contribution remains driven by Services at 58.2% of GDP (growing at 4.09%), Agriculture at 22.9% (growing at 2.89%), and Industry/LSM at 18.9% (rebounding at 10.37%). External stability has been supported by resilient worker remittances reaching USD 30.2 billion annually and IT/software exports exceeding USD 3.39 billion. However, household purchasing power remains constrained: according to the PBS Labour Force Survey, the national mean monthly wage stands at PKR 39,042, with profound wage disparities between formal urban corporate sectors and informal rural agricultural labor.
