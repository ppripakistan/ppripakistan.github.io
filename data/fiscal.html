Fiscal Data

Fiscal indicators for provincial-reform analysis.

Research note

PPRI separates own-source revenue, federal transfers, development expenditure, current expenditure and fiscal balances. Fiscal capacity should not be inferred from population or GDP alone.

Source standard

Indicators should be traceable to the original institution, reference period and definition. PPRI does not treat an unverified number as established evidence.
