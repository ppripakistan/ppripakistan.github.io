7th NFC Horizontal Sharing Formula

Criterion / Indicator Weight Objective & Description
Population 82.0% Equal per capita distribution baseline across all provinces.
Poverty & Backwardness 10.3% Equalization transfer based on official Multidimensional Poverty Index (MPI).
Revenue Collection / Generation 5.0% Incentive share reflecting provincial economic contribution and tax collection.
Inverse Population Density (IPD) 2.7% Compensates provinces with large territorial landmasses and low population densities.
Total Horizontal Formula 100.0% Determines individual provincial shares of the 57.5% divisible pool.
