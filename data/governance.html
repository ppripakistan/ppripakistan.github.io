Administrative Scale Disparity

In Punjab, a single Deputy Commissioner and District Police Officer oversee an average of 3.55 million citizens (surpassing 13 million in Lahore district alone). In contrast, a district administrator in Balochistan oversees an average of 438,000 citizens, spread across thousands of square kilometers. These differing administrative realities necessitate distinct governance models for territorial reform.
