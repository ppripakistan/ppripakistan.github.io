What Is the Literacy Rate in Pakistan (2025–2026)?

Pakistan's official national literacy rate is 62.8% (for individuals aged 10 and above), according to the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics (PBS). Province-wise: Punjab leads at 66.3%, followed by Sindh at 61.8%, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa at 55.1%, and Balochistan at 43.8%. Islamabad Capital Territory records the highest rate nationally at 84.0%. Nationally, the male literacy rate is 73.4% versus 51.9% for females.

The Urban vs. Rural Education Divide

Pakistan's aggregate literacy statistics conceal an acute spatial and demographic cleavage between metropolitan centers and rural tehsils. In urban Sindh (dominated by Karachi and Hyderabad), literacy averages 78.0%, whereas rural Sindh drops precipitously to 42.2%. The divide is most extreme among rural females: in rural Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, female literacy is 37.4%, while in rural Balochistan, fewer than 24% of adult women can read and write with basic comprehension.
