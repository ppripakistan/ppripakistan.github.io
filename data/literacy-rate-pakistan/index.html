What Is the Literacy Rate in Pakistan (HIES 2024–25)?

Pakistan's official national literacy rate is 63% (for population aged 10 and above), according to the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics (PBS) HIES 2024–25 Social Indicators Report. Across provinces: Punjab leads at 68%, followed by Sindh at 58%, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa at 58%, and Balochistan at 49%. Nationally, male literacy is 73% versus 54% for females, and urban literacy is 74% versus 55% in rural areas.

Methodological Note: HIES 2024–25 vs. Census 2023 (60.65%)

Do not mix survey and census series without noting their distinct methodologies. The sample-based PBS HIES 2024–25 reports rounded indicators (63% national age 10+ literacy), whereas the complete-enumeration PBS National Census Report 2023 recorded a 60.65% national literacy rate.

The Urban vs. Rural & Provincial Education Divide

In PBS HIES 2024–25, Pakistan's national urban literacy rate (74%) exceeds the rural rate (55%) by 19 percentage points, mirroring the national gender gap between males (73%) and females (54%). Across provinces, out-of-school rates among children aged 5–16 remain sharply elevated in Balochistan (45%) and Sindh (39%) compared to Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (28%) and Punjab (21%).
