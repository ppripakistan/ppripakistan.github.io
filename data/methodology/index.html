Data Observatory: Sources, Definitions & Methodology

How PPRI compiles, verifies, and maps official public statistics to search intents without fabrication or distortion.

Our Five Principles of Empirical Integrity

1. Primary Official Authority

We prioritize official Pakistani primary sources: Pakistan Bureau of Statistics (PBS), Finance Division, State Bank of Pakistan (SBP), and National Accounts. Secondary aggregators are strictly cross-checked.

2. Strict Definition Hygiene

We never conflate distinct statistical concepts. Survey estimates (HIES) are strictly separated from census enumerations. Nominal household income is not individual salary, and consumption quintiles are not poverty lines.

3. No Fabricated Numbers

If an official table has not yet been extracted (e.g. PBS Census district workbooks pending XLSX conversion), we explicitly state its pending status rather than guessing or interpolating figures.

4. Search Intent Transparency

Google Year in Search highlights trending queries, not total monthly search volume. We clearly distinguish between trending growth signals and absolute keyword volumes.
