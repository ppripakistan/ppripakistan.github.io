INFOGRAPHIC 04 / 16 Demographics • Gender & Territory Asymmetry

Female Gender Ratios & Agrarian Landholding Asymmetry

Male, female, and transgender distribution in relation to provincial land area shares

Key Policy Takeaway: Balochistan spans 43.6% of Pakistan's territory (347,190 km²) but carries only 6.2% of the population (14.89M) at 42.9 people/km². Conversely, Punjab concentrates 52.9% of citizens (127.69M) on 25.8% of land area (621.8 people/km²). Nationally, 20,190 transgender citizens were recorded.
Male: 51.48% Female: 48.51% Transgender: 20,190 Sex Ratio: 106.1
Source: Pakistan Bureau of Statistics, 7th Population & Housing Census 2023 Table 1 • PPRI
INFOGRAPHIC 10 / 16 Federal Demographics • 4-Decade Census Shifts

Multi-Census Provincial Demographic Shifts (1981–2023)

Decadal shifts in provincial demographic shares across 1981, 1998, 2017, and 2023 censuses

Key Policy Takeaway: Significant federal rebalancing: Punjab's demographic share contracted by -3.2% (56.1% in 1981 to 52.9% in 2023), while Balochistan grew from 5.1% to 6.2% (14.89M) and KP reached 16.9% following the FATA merger.
Punjab Shift: 56.1% → 52.9% Balochistan: 5.1% → 6.2% KP + FATA: 13.1% → 16.9%
Source: Pakistan Bureau of Statistics, Censuses of Pakistan (1981, 1998, 2017, 2023) • PPRI
INFOGRAPHIC 11 / 16 Spatial Transition • 135 Districts Scatter Matrix

135-District Urbanization & Population Density Quadrant Matrix

2D coordinate matrix: Urbanization Share (%) vs Density (people/km² on log scale) • Bubble radius sized by total population

Key Policy Takeaway: Resolves the 0.0% data key bug and maps all 141 districts into four structural archetypes: Dense Metros (Karachi >43,000/km², Lahore 7,294/km²), Dense Agrarian Hubs (Charsadda, Gujranwala peri-urban), Vast Arid Periphery (Chaghi 5.2/km², Washuk, Awaran), and Urbanized Frontier Outposts (Quetta 61.2% urban, Gwadar).
Total Districts Plotted: 141 Dense Metros (>70% Urban): 14 Districts Deep Agrarian (<15% Urban): 42 Districts Hyper-Sparse (<50/km²): 26 Districts
Source: Pakistan Bureau of Statistics, 7th Population & Housing Census 2023 Table 1 (141 Districts) • PPRI
INFOGRAPHIC 14 / 16 Demographic Trajectory • 70-Year Census

70-Year Intercensal Demographic Growth Trajectory (1951–2023)

Seven census milestones and intercensal annual growth rate trajectory

Key Policy Takeaway: Pakistan multiplied 7.2-fold in 72 years from 33.7 million in 1951 to 241.5 million in 2023. Annual growth currently stands at 2.55%, adding over 6.1 million people annually.
1951 Population: 33.7M 2023 Population: 241.5M Intercensal Growth: 2.55% p.a.
Source: Pakistan Bureau of Statistics, Censuses of Pakistan (1951–2023) • PPRI
