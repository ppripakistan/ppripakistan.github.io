Pakistan Population, Urbanization & City Scale: Executive Demographic Summary

Executive Overview: Official results from the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics (PBS) 7th Population and Housing Census 2023 record Pakistan's total population at 241,499,431 citizens, reflecting an average intercensal annual growth rate of 2.55% between 2017 and 2023. This demographic expansion represents an addition of over 33.8 million people in six years, placing acute administrative pressure on provincial service delivery, urban infrastructure, and administrative boundaries.

Demographic weight is heavily concentrated: Punjab contains 127,688,922 citizens (52.88%), Sindh contains 55,696,147 (23.06%), Khyber Pakhtunkhwa houses 40,856,097 (16.92%), Balochistan comprises 14,894,402 (6.17%), and Islamabad Capital Territory holds 2,363,863 (0.98%). Pakistan's urban population reached 93.75 million (38.82%), led by two megacities: Karachi Division at 20,382,881 citizens and Lahore District at 13,004,135 citizens. Pakistan now has 22 secondary cities exceeding 500,000 residents, yet municipal governance remains constrained under centralized provincial bureaucracies.

Demographics of Lahore vs. Secondary Cities in Punjab (2023 Census)

According to the official results of the 7th Population and Housing Census 2023 conducted by the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics (PBS), the demographics of Lahore reflect rapid urban concentration. The district of Lahore has reached an enumerated population of 13,004,135 citizens (13.0 million), solidifying its position as Pakistan's second megalopolis after Karachi (18.87M) and the 24th most populous city in the world.

Lahore's annual intercensal growth rate of 3.01% significantly outpaces Punjab's overall provincial growth rate of 2.53%. This demographic surge is driven by internal rural-to-urban migration from Southern and Central Punjab, attracted by the concentration of tertiary hospitals, elite universities, provincial administrative secretariats, and industrial employment.
