What is the population of Pakistan?

PBS Census source ↗

Pakistan’s population was 241,499,431 in the 2023 Population and Housing Census, according to the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics (PBS). This is the latest census count shown on this page—not a live 2026 population estimate. The 2017 census counted 207,684,626 people; the reported average annual growth rate between the two censuses was 2.55%.

Pakistan Population, Urbanization & City Scale: Executive Demographic Summary

Executive Overview: Official results from the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics (PBS) 7th Population and Housing Census 2023 record Pakistan's total population at 241,499,431 citizens, reflecting an average intercensal annual growth rate of 2.55% between 2017 and 2023. This demographic expansion represents an addition of over 33.8 million people in six years, placing acute administrative pressure on provincial service delivery, urban infrastructure, and administrative boundaries.

Demographic weight is heavily concentrated: Punjab contains 127,688,922 citizens (52.88%), Sindh contains 55,696,147 (23.06%), Khyber Pakhtunkhwa houses 40,856,097 (16.92%), Balochistan comprises 14,894,402 (6.17%), and Islamabad Capital Territory holds 2,363,863 (0.98%). For the urban-rural split, this page uses the PBS Census 2023 Table 1 series: 93,750,724 urban residents and 147,748,707 rural residents. City population figures must be interpreted using their published geographic definition; an urban locality is not the same as a district or division.

Demographics of Lahore vs. Secondary Cities in Punjab (2023 Census)

According to the official results of the 7th Population and Housing Census 2023 conducted by the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics (PBS), the demographics of Lahore reflect rapid urban concentration. The district of Lahore has reached an enumerated population of 13,004,135 citizens (13.0 million), but comparisons with Karachi depend on the geographic unit used in each source. Census locality, district, division and metropolitan-area figures are not interchangeable.

Lahore's annual intercensal growth rate of 3.01% significantly outpaces Punjab's overall provincial growth rate of 2.53%. This demographic surge is driven by internal rural-to-urban migration from Southern and Central Punjab, attracted by the concentration of tertiary hospitals, elite universities, provincial administrative secretariats, and industrial employment.

Quick answers

Pakistan Population: Frequently Asked Questions

What is the population of Pakistan according to the 2023 census?

The Pakistan Bureau of Statistics reports 241,499,431 people in the 2023 Population and Housing Census. This is a census count, not a live estimate for 2026.

What was Pakistan’s population in 2017?

The 2017 census counted 207,684,626 people in the Table 1 comparison series used on this page.

Which province has the largest population in Pakistan?

Punjab was the most populous province in the 2023 census, with 127,688,922 people, about 52.87% of the national total.

What is the population of Punjab in the 2023 census?

Punjab’s 2023 census population was 127,688,922.

What is the population of Sindh in the 2023 census?

Sindh’s 2023 census population was 55,696,147.

What is the population of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa in the 2023 census?

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa’s 2023 census population was 40,856,097.

What is the population of Balochistan in the 2023 census?

Balochistan’s 2023 census population was 14,894,402.

What is Pakistan’s population growth rate?

PBS reported an average annual growth rate of 2.55% between the 2017 and 2023 censuses. This historical rate is not a verified 2026 growth rate.

How many people live in urban and rural Pakistan?

The PBS Census 2023 Table 1 series used on this page reports 93,750,724 urban residents and 147,748,707 rural residents. Some PBS report tables show a slightly different split; use a named table series when comparing figures.

What percentage of Pakistan’s population lives in cities?

Using the PBS Census 2023 Table 1 series, 93,750,724 of 241,499,431 people were classified as urban—about 38.82%. The result depends on the census table’s urban/rural classification.

What is the population density of Pakistan?

Population density depends on the land-area denominator and geographic coverage used. Consult PBS Census 2023 Table 1 for official national and province-wise density figures rather than combining population counts with an unrelated area estimate.

What is the population of Karachi?

Karachi figures vary depending on whether the source reports an urban locality, district, division or wider metropolitan definition. Always compare the named geographic unit and exact PBS census table; these boundaries are not interchangeable.

What is the population of Lahore?

Lahore population figures may refer to the urban locality or Lahore District. State the unit and census table when citing a number; do not treat district and city populations as synonyms.

Does the 2023 census total shown here include Gilgit-Baltistan and Azad Jammu and Kashmir?

The 241,499,431 total on this page is the PBS Table 1 series covering the four provinces and Islamabad Capital Territory. Gilgit-Baltistan and Azad Jammu and Kashmir are not included in that five-area total.

Where can I download official Pakistan census population data?

Start with the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics Census portal and its detailed results and Excel tables. This page also provides a downloadable PPRI CSV derived from the cited source series.

Is 241.5 million Pakistan’s current population in 2026?

No. It is the enumerated population from the 2023 census. A 2026 population estimate is a different statistic and should be labelled with its projection source, reference date and method.
