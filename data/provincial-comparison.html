PPRI Data Observatory

Compare administrative units.

Use the same indicator across two units. The tool is descriptive; it does not produce a political ranking or recommendation.

How to read this: differences in population, density or urbanization describe structural conditions. They do not by themselves establish whether a province should be divided, retained or reorganized.

Indicator definitions

  • Density: population per square kilometre.
  • Urban proportion: share of the population classified as urban in the Census 2023 framework.
  • Annual growth: average annual population growth between 2017 and 2023 as reported by PBS.

Source: Pakistan Bureau of Statistics Census 2023 Table 1 and National Census Report 2023.
