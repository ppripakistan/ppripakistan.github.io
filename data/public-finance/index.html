Federal Budget Allocation & Sovereign Debt Servicing

Expenditure distribution across sovereign debt markup, defence, civil government, PSDP, and federal grants

INFOGRAPHIC 02 / 16 Public Debt • Solvency Pressure

The 8-Year Sovereign Public Debt Trajectory (FY18–FY26)

Escalation of domestic debt, external obligations, and annual debt servicing burden across all 9 fiscal years

Key Policy Takeaway: Pakistan's total public debt surged from PKR 24,953B in FY18 to PKR 83,285B in FY26 (+233.8%), while annual markup servicing expanded 6.5-fold from PKR 1,499B to PKR 9,775B.
Total Debt: PKR 83,285B Domestic Share: 69.1% External Share: 30.9%
Source: Ministry of Finance & SBP, Debt Bulletins (FY18–FY26) • PPRI
INFOGRAPHIC 03 / 16 Tax Policy • Revenue Concessions

Federal Tax Concessions & Revenue Foregone

FBR revenue foregone across Sales Tax, Customs duties, and Income Tax concessions

Key Policy Takeaway: Total federal tax concessions reach PKR 2,434.7B (2.32% of GDP), equivalent to 26.2% of actual tax collection. Sales tax exemptions under 6th Schedule represent over half of all foregone revenues.
Foregone Revenue: PKR 2,434.7B Sales Tax Loss: 50.8% Tax-to-GDP Ratio: 9.2%
Source: Federal Board of Revenue (FBR), Tax Expenditure Report 2025 • PPRI
INFOGRAPHIC 16 / 16 Provincial Fiscal & Security Disparities • 4 Provinces Multi-Dimensional Comparison

Provincial Resource Allocation, Debt Stock & Conflict Exposure Matrix

Cross-provincial comparison of total budgetary outlay, Annual Development Programmes (ADP), outstanding public debt, per capita allocations, and regional terrorism burden

Key Policy Takeaway: Severe structural disparities exist between fiscal concentration and conflict exposure: Punjab and Sindh command 75.9% of all provincial budget spending, while Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (63.1%) and Balochistan (29.1%) bear 92.2% of Pakistan's terrorism fatalities. Furthermore, while Balochistan receives the highest nominal budget per person (PKR 64,158), its infrastructure allocation per km² (PKR 0.92M/km²) is less than one-seventh of Sindh's (PKR 6.81M/km²), reflecting severe territorial underdevelopment.
National Total Outlay: PKR 11.21 Trillion Total Prov Development: PKR 2.54 Trillion KP + Balochistan Conflict Share: 92.2% Fatalities Highest Debt Stock: Punjab (PKR 1.685T)
Source: Provincial Finance Departments (White Papers FY25), DMU Debt Bulletins, CRSS Annual Security Reports (2023–24) • PPRI
