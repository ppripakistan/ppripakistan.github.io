Pakistan Governance, Demographics & Fast Facts Matrix: Executive Summary

Executive Overview: This rapid reference data matrix provides official, verified statistical benchmarks on Pakistan's constitutional structure, demographic scale, public finance, and territorial administration. Sourced directly from the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics 7th Census 2023, the 7th NFC Award, the Pakistan Economic Survey 2025–26, and the Constitution of Pakistan, these verified indicators support empirical research, civil service examination preparation, and public policy analysis.

Pakistan operates as a federal parliamentary republic established under the Constitution of 1973. It comprises 4 constitutional provinces, 1 federal territory, 33 administrative divisions, 146 districts (plus 24 districts across AJK and Gilgit-Baltistan), and 574 tehsils. The 18th Constitutional Amendment of 2010 devolved 47 federal subjects to provincial assemblies, but sub-provincial devolution under Article 140A remains incomplete across all four provinces.

For CSS Pakistan Affairs and Economics papers, examiners look for primary source attribution. Always cite the issuing government agency: "According to the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics 7th Population and Housing Census 2023..." rather than attributing the figure to a secondary website or newspaper.
