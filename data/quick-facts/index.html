Governance / Fiscal Federalism Ministry of Finance / 7th NFC Award

7th NFC Award Distribution Formula

Population: 82.0% Weight
Divisible Pool Weighting

Multi-criteria formula established in 2010: Population (82.0%), Poverty/Backwardness (10.3%), Revenue Collection (5.0%), and Inverse Population Density (2.7%). Provincial shares: Punjab 51.74%, Sindh 24.55%, KP 14.62% (excl. FATA), Balochistan 9.09%.

Examination & Research Citation Advice

For CSS Pakistan Affairs and Economics papers, examiners look for primary source attribution. Always cite the issuing government agency: "According to the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics 7th Population and Housing Census 2023..." rather than attributing the figure to a secondary website or newspaper. Use the 'Copy Citation' button on any card above to get exact, examiner-approved attributions.
