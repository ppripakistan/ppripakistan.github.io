Research programme

A connected set of institutional questions.

View research agenda
01 · Constitutional

Constitutional framework

Procedure, provincial consent, parliamentary action and the legal baseline.

 02 · Administrative

Administrative design

Institutions, headquarters, civil services, courts and service networks.

 03 · Fiscal

Fiscal federalism

Revenue, transfers, expenditure responsibilities and transition finance.

 04 · Data

Demography & scale

Population, area, density, administrative units and data definitions.

 05 · Local

Local government

Decentralization below the provincial level and the relationship between tiers.

 06 · Transition

Implementation pathways

Sequencing, assets, personnel, institutions and continuity of public services.
