Research programme

Six dimensions of reform.

PPRI separates the debate into constitutional, administrative, fiscal, demographic, geographic and local-government questions.

01 · Constitution

Constitutional framework

Legal pathways, constitutional requirements and institutional authority.

02 · Administration

Administrative design

Departments, personnel, institutions and public-service delivery.

03 · Finance

Fiscal framework

Revenue, expenditure, transfers and transition costs.

 04 · Demography

Population & representation

Population distribution, density and representation questions.

05 · Geography

Maps & territorial structure

Administrative geography, boundaries and spatial analysis.

06 · Local government

Decentralization

How provincial reform interacts with power at the local level.
