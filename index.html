PPRI approach

Evidence before conclusions.

Arguments are separated from facts, sources are identified, and competing reform models are examined on institutional and fiscal implications.

Research programme

Six questions. One connected system.

01 · Constitutional

Can it be done lawfully?

Constitutional amendment, provincial consent and institutional procedure.

02 · Administrative

Can institutions be built?

Departments, civil services, courts, capital locations and service networks.

03 · Fiscal

Can it be financed?

Revenue, transfers, assets, liabilities and transition expenditure.

04 · Evidence

What do the numbers show?

Population, density, geography and future socioeconomic indicators.

05 · Geography

Where are the boundaries?

Current official geography and clearly labelled analytical scenarios.

06 · Decentralization

What should stay local?

Provincial restructuring and local empowerment should be tested together.
