Spatial Cartography & Territorial Boundary Models

PPRI cartography rigorously separates official administrative reality from analytical reform scenarios. Explore interactive district boundaries, geographic scales, and custom territorial projections.

Cartographic Classification Standards

How PPRI categorizes spatial models
Layer 1

Official Atlas

Current de jure boundaries: 4 provinces, ICT, and 144 administrative districts (168 total incl. 14 GB & 10 AJK, Admin Geography 2023; 135 tabulated in Census 2023).

Layer 2

Analytical Scenarios

Hypothetical exploratory boundary models testing demographic parity without prescriptive endorsement.

Layer 3

Choropleth Analytics

Spatially mapped census indicators: density, urbanization rate, travel distance, and district GDP.

*Statistical Note on District Counts: 135 officially tabulated Census 2023 provincial districts (PBS Census 2023); 144 gazetted districts across the 4 constitutional provinces + ICT (41 Punjab, 30 Sindh, 36 KP, 36 Balochistan, 1 ICT); 168 total administrative districts across all 38 divisions when including Gilgit-Baltistan (14) and Azad Jammu & Kashmir (10) — Admin Geography 2023 baseline (141 editable ADM3 polygons in the Scenario Lab GIS layer).
