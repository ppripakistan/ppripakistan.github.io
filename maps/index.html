*Statistical Note on District Counts: 135 officially tabulated Census 2023 districts across the 4 constitutional provinces and Islamabad Capital Territory; ~164–170 administrative districts when including post-2023 provincial notifications, sub-divisions, and the administrative territories of Gilgit-Baltistan and Azad Jammu & Kashmir.
