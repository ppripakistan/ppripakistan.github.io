Scenario composition

What is included in the first-pass model?

The primary layer uses district/division building blocks. Federal-territory concepts are visualized separately where they overlap the primary layer.

What changed?Move a district in Edit mode to see the live change here.

Primary territorial layer

West Punjab · South Punjab · Bahawalpur · Potohar · Urban Sindh · Rural Sindh · Mehran · Balochistan · Makran · Khyber · South KPK · Hazara · Kohistan.

Federal overlays

Islamabad · Gilgit-Baltistan · Azad Kashmir · Karachi · Gwadar. These are treated as overlays in this scenario so the model can test a federal-status concept without double-counting geography.

Why district-based?

It makes every proposed boundary traceable to existing administrative units instead of drawing arbitrary polygons. Later versions can move from a first-pass model to formally reviewed district assignments.

What the tool does not claim

It does not establish constitutional validity, fiscal viability, public support, legal status or an official PPRI position. Those require separate research.

Next analytical layer

Population, area, fiscal exposure, service access, infrastructure, water systems, representation and transition costs can be added once district-level datasets are verified.
