Cartographic Scenario Modeling: Pakistan Administrative Scale & Reorganization

Executive Overview: The PPRI Cartographic Scenario Lab provides interactive spatial modeling tools to examine sub-national administrative restructuring in Pakistan. Based on 2023 Census micro-data across Pakistan's 146 districts and 574 tehsils, the primary illustrative scenario (PPRI-01) evaluates a polycentric 13-unit administrative model designed to optimize citizen proximity, equalize demographic weight, and streamline public expenditure.

Under the baseline 4-province model, average provincial population exceeds 60.3 million citizens—an extreme global outlier compared to India (average 36M), Nigeria (5.8M), the United States (6.7M), or Germany (5.2M). Reorganizing Pakistan into 13 autonomous provincial units reduces average provincial population to 18.5 million citizens, eliminates multi-day travel distances to provincial secretariats, and establishes a balanced Senate and NFC divisible pool distribution without altering external federal borders.

Why district-based?

It makes every proposed boundary traceable to existing administrative units instead of drawing arbitrary polygons. Later versions can move from a first-pass model to formally reviewed district assignments.

What the tool does not claim

It does not establish constitutional validity, fiscal viability, public support, legal status or an official PPRI position. Those require separate research.

Next analytical layer
