About PPRI

The Pakistan Provincial Reform Institute is a research platform focused on provincial reform, federalism, decentralization, public administration and institutional design in Pakistan.

What PPRI researches

Provincial reform

Questions around territorial organization, administrative scale and representation.

Federalism

Constitutional roles, intergovernmental relations and fiscal arrangements.

Local government

Political, administrative and financial devolution below the provincial level.
