October 18, 2026 · Milestone Release

PPRI Expands Policy Archive to 25 Articles & Completes Subnational Governance Series

ISLAMABAD — The Pakistan Provincial Reform Institute (PPRI) has reached a major institutional milestone with the publication of its 25th analytical policy paper. The comprehensive archive covers five thematic pillars: administrative scale, fiscal federalism and NFC formulas, Article 140A municipal devolution, regional case studies (including South Punjab, Bahawalpur, Hazara, and Karachi), and empirical spatial rubrics.

October 17, 2026 · Observatory Launch

Central Data Observatory & Knowledge Map Goes Live Across 10 Thematic Domains

ISLAMABAD — PPRI today unveiled its upgraded Central Data Observatory, integrating 10 empirical domains spanning population demography (Census 2023), household economics (HIES 2024–25), public finance and 7th NFC simulations, literacy benchmarks, public health infrastructure, and digital access. The observatory features live SVG distribution charts, interactive fiscal simulators, and direct cross-links to constitutional reform models.

October 16, 2026 · Data Matrix Release

164-District Administrative Database & 2026 CSS Fast-Facts Matrix Released for Open Access

ISLAMABAD — PPRI has published two extensive open-access resources for researchers, civil service candidates, and journalists: the comprehensive 164-District Explorer, detailing 2023 Census populations and secretariat distances, and the 2026 CSS Fast-Facts Matrix, consolidating verified administrative, fiscal, and demographic indicators with official government citations.

October 10, 2026 · Research Series

PPRI Releases 5-Report Subnational Inequality & Fiscal Decentralization Series

ISLAMABAD / LAHORE — PPRI has released a five-part flagship research report series examining subnational inequality, administrative friction, and the economics of decentralization. The series includes deep-dive investigations into household purchasing power under inflation, National Corruption Perception Survey (NCPS) administrative bribe burdens, the Pakistan Bureaucracy Cost Index, district-level basic service disparities, and an empirical evaluation of local government vs. centralized provincial ADP spending.

Access 5-Report Series → Read Flagship Paper
October 1, 2026 · Official Release

PPRI Launches Interactive 16-Unit Spatial Scenario Lab & GIS Model

ISLAMABAD — The Pakistan Provincial Reform Institute (PPRI) today launched its interactive GIS-backed Scenario Lab, allowing researchers, journalists, and policymakers to model hypothetical 12-unit and 16-unit administrative architectures for Pakistan. The tool simulates live demographic distributions, capital proximities, and parliamentary representation across all 164 districts.

September 15, 2026 · Publication Notice

Flagship Policy Paper: "The Provincial Reorganization of Pakistan" Published

LAHORE / ISLAMABAD — PPRI has published its first comprehensive policy paper (PPRI-PP-001) examining the constitutional, fiscal, administrative, and local-government dimensions of territorial reform. The paper argues that provincial reorganization and Article 140A devolution must be studied conjointly rather than treated as competing alternatives.

Digital Census 2023 Provincial Baseline Dataset Released for Open Access

ISLAMABAD — In accordance with its open-data research charter, PPRI has released machine-readable CSV and JSON baselines of the 7th Population and Housing Census (2023), providing researchers with standardized demographic, urbanization, and density benchmarks across all provinces and divisions.
