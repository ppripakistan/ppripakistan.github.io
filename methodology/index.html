Methodology

Research & Data Methodology

Standards for evidence, data and policy analysis.

Source hierarchy

Tier 1: Pakistani constitutional, legislative and official statistical sources. Tier 2: established international institutions. Tier 3: peer-reviewed research, universities and established think tanks.

Data treatment

Record source, date, reference period, geography, unit, transformation and limitations.

Claims

Separate documented facts, PPRI calculations, analytical interpretation and recommendations.

Public opinion

Survey results should disclose recruitment, sample size, dates, geography, wording and limitations.
