Methodology

Sentiment & Citizen-Experience Analysis

How PPRI handles citizen perception data, complaint evidence, media discourse, and the distinction between administrative statistics and genuine public opinion.

Terminology note: The phrase "sentimental analysis" is treated here as citizen sentiment / public-perception analysis, not an unsupported claim that PPRI has run a nationally representative natural-language sentiment model.

Data Types

1. Survey Sentiment

Direct survey responses from structured instruments:

  • Transparency International Pakistan NCPS 2025/2023 — corruption perceptions and reported experiences
  • Historical citizen satisfaction surveys — around the local-government/devolution period

These are treated as the strongest form of citizen-experience evidence because they use defined sampling, standardised questions and measurable confidence intervals.

2. Administrative Grievance Signals

Institutional complaint and redressal data from:

  • Pakistan Citizen's Portal
  • Wafaqi Mohtasib
  • Ombudsman Punjab

Administrative grievance data is not a representative public-opinion survey. It reflects people who used complaint channels and therefore measures revealed administrative demand and friction, not population-wide sentiment.

3. Media Discourse

Pakistani media is useful for:

  • Identifying recurring public arguments
  • Capturing regional grievances
  • Identifying political claims
  • Finding historical quotations

Media rhetoric is not treated as public opinion unless backed by survey evidence.

Analytical Rules

PPRI research never claims:

Prohibited claim Why it is wrong
"Pakistanis feel X" based only on a newspaper column Media is not a representative sample
"Most people are corrupt/bribed" based on perception survey percentages Perception ≠ audited reality; reported ≠ universal
"Resolved complaint = satisfied citizen" Administrative status ≠ independent satisfaction verification
"Social-media sentiment = national public opinion" Platform users are not representative; algorithms shape visibility

Required precise language

  • "Respondents reported…"
  • "The portal records…"
  • "The survey found…"
  • "Media discourse frequently frames the issue as…"

Recommended Future PPRI Text-Analysis Project

For a future primary-research project, PPRI should collect a clearly defined corpus:

  • Citizen Portal complaint texts where legally and ethically accessible
  • Anonymised consultation submissions
  • Ombudsman complaint descriptions where available
  • Pakistani news reporting on provincial/local-government reform

Classify text into actionable themes instead of a simple positive/negative score:

Delay
Cost
Bribery
Non-response
Documentation
Harassment
Service failure
Infrastructure
Discrimination
LG demand
Regional autonomy

The codebook and sampling method must be published alongside any results.
