Institutional Policy & Data Privacy

Privacy Charter & Data Governance Policy

The Pakistan Provincial Reform Institute (PPRI) is committed to rigorous standards of digital privacy, academic transparency, and data protection. This charter outlines how we handle user data across our static website, interactive tools, and public consultations.

Core Privacy Commitments

Effective Date: October 2026
Principle 01

Zero Commercial Trackers

PPRI does not deploy commercial advertising networks, social tracking pixels, or monetized user profiling scripts anywhere on this website.

Principle 02

Local-First Architecture

Interactive tools—including the Scenario Lab and Survey Engine—execute calculations entirely in your browser using local client memory.

Principle 03

Transparent Submissions

Drafts stay in your browser (`sessionStorage`) until submitted; completed community surveys are routed via FormSubmit.co to PPRI's research mailbox (`ppripakistan@gmail.com`).

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not require user accounts, passwords, phone numbers, or mandatory personal profiles to access any of PPRI's policy papers, empirical datasets, scenario modeling tools, or constitutional libraries. All research materials are public and open-access.

2. Interactive Calculators & Local Browser Storage

When using our Scenario Lab, the website uses browser localStorage to preserve your active scenario configurations across browser reloads. When completing a Community Survey, in-progress answers, optional region selection, and optional comments are saved in browser sessionStorage so you do not lose work if a submission fails or times out; the draft is cleared only after the submission service confirms receipt.

3. Community Surveys & Consultation Submissions

When you click Submit on a community survey or submit a consultation memorandum:

  • Where submissions go: Survey responses (selected answers, optional province/region, and optional free-text comments) are transmitted via the third-party FormSubmit.co relay to PPRI's institutional mailbox (ppripakistan@gmail.com). Fallback email links also address ppripakistan@gmail.com directly.
  • Who can access them: Submissions are accessed only by authorized PPRI research and editorial staff who manage ppripakistan@gmail.com.
  • How long they are kept: Survey and consultation submissions are retained indefinitely for longitudinal archival policy research unless deletion is requested.
  • How to request deletion: You may request deletion of any submitted response or consultation memo at any time by emailing ppripakistan@gmail.com with the survey name, approximate submission date/region, or consultation details.
  • Publication & anonymity: We do not sell, rent, or trade correspondent information. Consultation submissions cited in published policy digests are cited only with the author's explicit written consent, or anonymized upon request.

4. Hosting Infrastructure & Server Logs

The PPRI website is hosted via GitHub Pages static edge infrastructure. Standard server request logs (including anonymized IP addresses, browser user-agent strings, and request timestamps) are processed by GitHub to ensure infrastructural reliability, defend against denial-of-service attacks, and maintain network security.

5. Inquiries Regarding Data Privacy

For questions regarding PPRI's digital data protection practices, or to request the deletion of any voluntarily submitted survey response or consultation memo, please contact our Institutional Secretariat at ppripakistan@gmail.com.
