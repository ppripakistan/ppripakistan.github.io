Reform Architecture & Roadmap

Pakistan Provincial Governance & Administrative Reform Hub

What would changing Pakistan's internal borders actually solve? PPRI breaks down the reform question into a structured 3-phase institutional roadmap spanning constitutional law, administrative capacity, fiscal equalization, and municipal devolution.

The 3-Phase Reform Lifecycle

Sequential methodology for subnational restructuring
Phase A

Foundations & Models

Evaluating demographic scale, representation deficits, and establishing Model A through D taxonomy.

Phase B

Institutional Pillars

Aligning constitutional amendments, judicial/civil administration, and NFC revenue equalization formulas.

Phase C

Execution & Devolution

Sequencing the 7-stage operational transition and securing Article 140A municipal local government powers.
