Decentralization

New provinces are not the only reform question.

Article 140A makes local government a constitutional part of Pakistan's governance architecture. PPRI examines provincial restructuring and local devolution together.

What Article 140A establishes

The Constitution requires each province to establish a local-government system and devolve political, administrative and financial responsibility and authority to elected local representatives. The Election Commission of Pakistan is constitutionally responsible for conducting local-government elections.

Why this matters

One argument for territorial restructuring is that government can become distant from citizens as administrative scale grows. Local government addresses the same distance problem through a different mechanism: authority is moved downward rather than a new provincial tier being created.

Research test Does territorial restructuring improve identified outcomes? Does devolution improve identified outcomes?

The comparison describes institutional mechanisms; it does not select a preferred model.
