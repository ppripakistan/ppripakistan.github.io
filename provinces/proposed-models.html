Scenario framework

Compare models before drawing conclusions.

PPRI's model-neutral framework separates the governance objective from the institutional mechanism used to pursue it.

Model A

Existing provinces + stronger local government

Test whether political, administrative and financial devolution to elected local governments can address identified problems.

Model B

Additional provinces

Test whether territorial restructuring improves representation, access and administration sufficiently to justify its institutional and fiscal costs.

Model C

Provincial + local reform

Examine a combined architecture in which territorial change is paired with a defined local-government settlement.

Model D

Other decentralization

Consider administrative, fiscal, metropolitan or functional arrangements without changing provincial boundaries.

Comparison rule: PPRI should publish the indicators and trade-offs behind each model rather than declaring an automatic winner.
