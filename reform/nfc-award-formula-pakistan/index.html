7th NFC Award Formula: Provincial Shares & Weights

A complete reference guide to Pakistan's fiscal resource-sharing mechanism: the vertical split between federation and provinces, the 4-factor horizontal distribution weights, statutory provincial shares, and dated 11th NFC developments.

1. How the Pool Is Divided Between Tiers (Vertical Split)

Of the Federal Divisible Pool, 57.5% goes to the four provinces and 42.5% remains with the Federal Government (with 1% of the total pool allocated to KP for war-on-terror costs prior to horizontal division). Article 160(3A) bars reducing the provincial share below 57.5%.

2. How the Provincial Share Is Divided (Horizontal Weights)

The 57.5% provincial pool uses four weighted criteria—Population (82.0%), Poverty & Backwardness (10.3%), Revenue Collection/Generation (5.0%), and Inverse Population Density (2.7%)—yielding Punjab 51.74%, Sindh 24.55%, KP 14.62%, and Balochistan 9.09%.

1. The 4-Factor Horizontal Distribution Formula

Prior to the 7th NFC Award (signed in December 2009 and enacted from FY 2010–11), federal revenues were distributed strictly on a single criterion: population (100%). The 7th Award introduced a historic multi-criteria formula:

Criterion Formula Weight Primary Beneficiary Province Measurement Source
Population 82.0% Punjab (52.9% population share) Official National Census Counts
Poverty & Backwardness 10.3% Khyber Pakhtunkhwa & Balochistan PBS / Planning Commission Multidimensional Poverty Index
Revenue Collection / Generation 5.0% Sindh (Metropolitan Karachi collection hub) Federal Board of Revenue (FBR) collection records
Inverse Population Density (Land Area) 2.7% Balochistan (43.6% of national area, low density) Survey of Pakistan territorial surface area data

2. Provincial Statutory Shares Under the 7th NFC Award

Applying the 4-factor weighting yields the following statutory entitlement for each province from the provincial divisible pool (57.5% of gross divisible taxes):

Province 7th NFC Statutory Share Pre-7th NFC Share (Population-only) Net Change
Punjab 51.74% 53.01% −1.27% (Voluntary reduction to build national consensus)
Sindh 24.55% 24.94% −0.39%
Khyber Pakhtunkhwa 14.62% 13.82% +0.80% (Plus 1.0% special war-on-terror pool deduction)
Balochistan 9.09% 7.18% +1.91% (Guaranteed statutory minimum baseline)

3. The Article 160(3A) Constitutional Ratchet

Under Article 160(3A) of the Constitution of Pakistan, enacted via the 18th Constitutional Amendment in 2010:

"The share of the Provinces in each subsequent National Finance Commission Award shall not be less than the share given to the Provinces in the previous Award."

This constitutional clause prevents the federal government from unilaterally cutting provincial transfer percentages (57.5%) in subsequent NFC negotiations, making fiscal decentralization structurally permanent unless amended by a two-thirds parliamentary majority.

4. Dated Official Transfers & 11th NFC Commission Developments

  • Statutory Formula Baseline (December 2009 / FY2010–11): The 7th NFC Award remains legally operative under Article 160 until a successor Award is consensus-approved.
  • Q1 FY2026–27 Official Transfers (Jul–Sep 2026, Finance Division, Provisional): Federal divisible pool disbursements totaled Rs 1.895 trillion (Rs 1,895.24 billion) — Punjab Rs 942.72 billion, Sindh Rs 479.29 billion, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Rs 314.27 billion, and Balochistan Rs 158.95 billion.   • 11th NFC Commission Constituted (22 August 2025): The President constituted the 9-member 11th NFC under Article 160(1), chaired by Federal Finance Minister Senator Muhammad Aurangzeb.
  • Inaugural 11th NFC Session (4 December 2025): Held in Islamabad to deliberate divisible pool weights, ex-FATA/merged districts integration, and National Fiscal Pact targets. As of November 2026, no replacement Award has been finalized.
