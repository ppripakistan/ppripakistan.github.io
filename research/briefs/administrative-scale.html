Policy Brief · PPRI-PB-001

Administrative Scale & Representation

Questions for evaluating the size, reach and representation of provincial institutions.

September 2026PPRI

Purpose

This brief provides a framework for examining whether territorial scale affects representation, administrative reach and service delivery.

Research questions

  • How does population size relate to administrative accessibility?
  • What institutional scale is fiscally sustainable?
  • How does territorial organization affect representation?
  • What alternatives can achieve similar goals?
Research note: findings should be supported by comparable indicators and identified sources.
