Policy Brief · PPRI-PB-001

Administrative Scale & Representation

Questions for evaluating whether administrative size, political access and service delivery justify territorial or institutional reform.

The question

Population size and geographic scale can shape how far citizens must travel to access provincial institutions, how resources are coordinated and how political representation is organized. But smaller units are not automatically more effective.

Five tests

  1. Access: Are public institutions physically and administratively accessible?
  2. Representation: Are communities represented in institutions that make relevant decisions?
  3. Capacity: Can the proposed unit sustain competent institutions?
  4. Fiscal sustainability: Can recurring responsibilities be financed?
  5. Accountability: Does the reform create clearer responsibility for outcomes?

Research implication

PPRI recommends measuring these questions directly rather than using population alone as a proxy for governance quality.
