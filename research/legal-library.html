How to use this library

Start with the official constitutional text, then consult legislation, rules, notifications and institutional material relevant to the specific question. PPRI summaries are guides and should not substitute for the primary legal text.

Primary-source rule: where a legal proposition is material to a PPRI publication, the publication should cite the relevant constitutional or statutory provision directly.
