The Provincial Reorganization of Pakistan

Constitutional, Administrative, Fiscal & Governance Considerations

September 2026Version 1.0PPRI
Download PDFRead online

Executive Summary

This publication establishes a framework for examining proposals concerning the territorial and institutional organization of Pakistan. It separates constitutional questions from administrative, fiscal, demographic and governance questions.

Scope: this Phase 2 page is a publication template. Replace placeholder analysis with fully sourced research before publication.

1. Problem definition

Research should examine how subnational scale affects representation, administrative effectiveness, fiscal management, service delivery and accountability.

2. Constitutional framework

The final paper should identify the constitutional provisions governing provinces, boundaries, legislative authority and institutional process using authoritative primary sources.

3. Administrative analysis

Possible effects include departments, civil-service structures, courts, police and other institutions. Transition sequencing and duplication of costs require explicit analysis.

4. Fiscal analysis

Assess revenue capacity, expenditure responsibilities, transfers, start-up costs and long-term fiscal sustainability.

5. Alternative models

Compare provincial reorganization with stronger local government, administrative decentralization, metropolitan governance and reforms within existing provincial boundaries.

6. Implementation framework

If a model is recommended, implementation should be divided into legal, administrative, fiscal, institutional and transition phases with measurable milestones.

7. Conclusion

The final paper should distinguish documented facts from recommendations and clearly identify uncertainty.
