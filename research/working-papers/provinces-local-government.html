Provinces & Local Government

Why provincial restructuring and local devolution should be examined together.

September 2026Working Paper

Abstract

This working paper examines the relationship between provincial organization and local government, focusing on whether territorial restructuring changes where decision-making power actually resides.

Research agenda

The full paper should compare institutional responsibilities, fiscal authority, elected representation and service delivery across relevant models.
