Working Paper · PPRI-WP-001

Provinces & Local Government

A comparative framework for territorial restructuring and constitutional local-government devolution.

Research question

Which governance problems are best addressed by creating a new province, and which are better addressed by devolving authority to elected local governments?

Institutional distinction

Provincial reorganization changes the number and boundaries of provincial units. Local-government reform changes the distribution of authority within the existing federal-provincial structure. Article 140A gives local government a distinct constitutional basis.

Proposed empirical tests

Compare travel/access to government, service-delivery indicators, fiscal transfers, administrative staffing, local revenue, institutional responsiveness and accountability mechanisms.

Next research step

Build comparable district-level datasets and examine whether identified governance outcomes correlate with administrative scale after controlling for socioeconomic and geographic differences.
